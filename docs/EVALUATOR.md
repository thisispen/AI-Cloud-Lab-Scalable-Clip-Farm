# Creative evaluation rubric — reject low-quality videos before publishing

Score each category 0–5 (0 = absent or severe failure, 3 = acceptable, 5 = outstanding). These ratings need human judgment or an agent with **access to the actual rendered video and audio**. Do not self-certify solely from a script or raw shot prompts.

1. **Opening hook**: does the first 1–3 seconds establish a concrete reason to keep watching without clickbait?
2. **Narrative clarity**: a viewer can identify topic, stakes and payoff; claims make sense without needing prior context.
3. **Visual quality**: no warped hands/product details, flicker, inconsistent faces, misaligned logos, unnatural movement or gratuitous AI imagery.
4. **Editorial rhythm**: each cut has a purpose; no dead air; shot lengths and music accentuate the idea.
5. **Sound**: intelligible narration, balanced effects and music, correct timing and no audio clipping.
6. **Brand credibility**: believable, distinctive voice and verified facts, product looks like the real product; no fake customer outcomes.
7. **Platform fit**: correct aspect, compelling on silent autoplay, captions legible in the safe zone, native format.
8. **Goal alignment**: CTA fits the offer, audience and funnel; avoids promoting a meaningless metric.

**Blocking issues regardless of score**: unapproved likeness/voice usage; unlicensed assets; misleading endorsement, price or product claim; undisclosed AI media where required; unreadable captions; missing approved destination link; false before/after evidence; platform policy violation.

## Creative-agent job specification

Ask Codex or your chosen creative agent to work in phases. Submit human-approved script and shot plan to the chosen generator only after a dollar limit is explicitly approved.

- Phase A: propose three distinctly different hooks and a structured shot list; explain why each shot contributes to viewer understanding or conversion.
- Phase B: critique against the rubric as a **hypothesis**, note missing sources and footage, then request missing proof/reference images before expensive generation.
- Phase C: generate a small number of **authorized** short test shots using least-expensive suitable provider; log spend and acceptance/rejection reasons.
- Phase D: assemble draft MP4 in FFmpeg; present to reviewer for the required actual-video/audio evaluation. Report technical checks separately from subjective quality.
- Phase E: produce final platform variants and metadata only after passing blockers and approval. Never spend on ads or publish automatically.

## Reviewer prompt

> Review the attached actual video and transcript for TikTok / Reels / Facebook ads / YouTube. Do not flatter the creator. Grade 8 categories 0–5 with time-coded evidence; distinguish observed problems from hypothesized retention effects. Flag all hard blockers. Suggest the five highest-impact editing changes with the smallest extra generation cost. Do not infer conversion or virality without testing and platform analytics.
